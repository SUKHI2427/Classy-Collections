A Neighbourhood Clothing Haven with a Classy Heart

Classy Collections was founded on a simple, timeless principle: exceptional clothing should make you feel confident, stylish, and celebrated without unreasonable boutique prices. Located prominently beside Manjunatha Kalyana Mantapa in Vajarahalli, our superstore has grown into the primary fashion destination for South Bengaluru families.

Every single piece on our hangers is hand-inspected for rich fabric texture, breatheable weave, impeccable stitching, and contemporary cuts. From festive silk sarees and designer Anarkalis for weddings to crisp cotton shirts for men and adorable celebration sets for kids, one relaxed visit equips the entire family.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where exactly is Classy Collections located?

We are situated at No. 34, right next to the landmark Manjunatha Kalyana Mantapa in Vajarahalli, near Kanakapura Main Road, Bengaluru. Ample two-wheeler and four-wheeler parking is available nearby.

Can I verify size availability before visiting?

Yes, absolutely! You can call us directly at 90353 86009 or send us a WhatsApp message with the outfit style or size you require. Our staff will check shelf stock immediately.

Do you cater clothing for the entire family?

Yes. Our 8 spacious aisles cover Women’s ethnic & western wear, Men’s formal shirts & trousers, Kids' celebration sets, and teenage casuals under one roof.

What are your operating days and timings?

We are open 7 days a week from 9:30 AM to 9:30 PM to accommodate working families and weekend wedding shoppers.
