Our Story

A neighbourhood shop with a classy heart

Classy Collections began with a simple belief: good clothes should feel special without feeling out of reach. Tucked beside Manjunatha Kalyana Mantapa in Vajarahalli, we have grown into a place families trust for festivals, weddings and every day in between.

Every piece on our racks is chosen with care, for fit, fabric and finish. Our customers have rated us 4.9 out of 5 across 56 reviews, and that is the story we are proudest of.

Walk in, take your time, and let us help you find the look that feels like you.
